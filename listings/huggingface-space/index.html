02 / Self-improvement system

Proposed full stack; only the baseline selector and context compiler are implemented here
Learning beyond LLMs

Rank. Predict. Experiment. Verify.

  • Supervised ML: acceptance, cost and escalation prediction with time-split calibration.
  • Unsupervised ML: cohort discovery, anomaly detection and concept drift.
  • Graph ML: temporal paths and relation-aware candidate ranking.
  • Causal inference: randomized comparisons and incremental outcome lift.
  • Bandits: constrained selection among approved policies; synthetic UCB1 is implemented.
  • RL/planning: sandbox only, with fidelity and safety gates before any pilot.
